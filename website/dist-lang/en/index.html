USB wired

PTP-compatible cameras connected with a USB-C (OTG) cable. Supports major makers: Nikon, Canon, Sony, Fujifilm, Panasonic, OM System (Olympus), Pentax, Ricoh, Leica and more.

Broad support
Wi-Fi wireless

Wireless (Wi-Fi) connection works with cameras that support standard PTP/IP. Models confirmed in real use carry a Wi-Fi badge in the list, and the set keeps growing. Any camera without wireless connects right away over USB.
