    Will my camera work?

    If your camera supports USB (PTP) or Wi-Fi STA (router) mode, it works with CamCon.

    USB wired

    PTP-compatible cameras connected with a USB-C (OTG) cable. Supports major makers: Nikon, Canon, Sony, Fujifilm, Panasonic, OM System (Olympus), Pentax, Ricoh, Leica and more.

    Broad support
    Wi-Fi wireless

    Wireless (Wi-Fi) connection currently supports the Nikon Z series (Z6 and Z8 verified on real hardware). Canon, Sony and Fujifilm wireless is under verification. Support is expanding. Other makers can connect right away over USB.

    Nikon verifiedOthers expanding

        Two ways to connect

        1. Connect cable

          Connect your phone and camera with a USB-C (OTG) cable.

        2. Allow access

          Allow the USB connection permission in the app.

        3. Start shooting

          Once it's recognized and connected, start shooting right away.

        Permission needed: USB device access (allowed once on connection)
        1. Same network

          Connect the camera and phone to the same router.

        2. Select camera

          Search for and select the camera in the app.

        3. Approve on camera

          On the first connection, approve "Allow connection" on the camera body. After that it reconnects automatically.

        Permissions needed: Location (to scan the network), Notifications (for background receiving)
